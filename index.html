Tier guide
Credit conditions
A separate signal, not part of the score above: whether corporate credit spreads are widening fast enough to suggest genuine financial-system stress, rather than just an expensive market. Based on the Baa–10Y spread's 6-month change.
How to read it: this is a warning light, not a vote in the composite score above. The three inputs measure how expensive markets are; credit conditions measure whether the financial system itself is under strain. Valuation can look calm while credit quietly deteriorates — as it did heading into 2008 — so when this flag moves to Watch or Elevated, it's worth weighing more heavily than the tier reading alone.
